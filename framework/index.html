Gwirio Trust Framework

The Gwirio Trust Framework

The Gwirio Trust Framework describes how trust can be established, represented, exchanged and consumed across digital ecosystems.

1

Trust Authority

An organisation that can authoritatively validate identity, authority, consent, legitimacy or another defined claim.

2

Trust Assertion

A validated claim originating from an authoritative source and carrying sufficient context to determine whether it can be relied upon.

3

Trust Exchange

The mechanisms through which trusted claims or outcomes can be exchanged between authorised participants.

4

Trust Consumer

An organisation, individual or system that relies upon a trust outcome.

5

Trust Outcome

The trusted result produced when relevant claims, evidence, controls and context have been evaluated.

6

Trust Decision

The human or automated decision supported by the resulting trusted outcome.

Where the Trust Decision Engine fits

The Trust Decision Engine is not simply another step after Trust Outcome. It is a capability within Trust Infrastructure — it evaluates Trust Assertions, evidence, policy, context, authority, consent, validity and governance, and contributes to producing a Trust Outcome.

Described conceptually, the architecture runs:

Trust Authorities → Trust Assertions → Trust Infrastructure → Trust Outcomes → Trust Consumers → Decisions

with Trust Exchange operating across organisational boundaries throughout. The six-step flow shown above is the high-level conceptual view; the Trust Decision Engine is one of the capabilities operating inside the Trust Infrastructure stage of it, not a seventh step after Trust Outcome.
