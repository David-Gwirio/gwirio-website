Trust Infrastructure

AI is everywhere
Machines are talking to machines
Transactions are happening faster and without human intervention.

Can we trust the rules, the processes and the machines?

How do we enhance trust in our digital world?

  • How do we identify fraud?
  • How do we identify someone behind an action or social account?
  • What information can we rely on when everything is digital?
  • How do we manage risk and know what a system is allowed to process, check and verify?
  • Who is accountable when something goes wrong?

Keep in mind that traditional digital systems were largely built around:

People→Systems→People

We are now rapidly moving toward

People→Systems→Systems→Systems→People, Products & Risk

More decisions are being made by systems.

More information is being processed without human intervention.

More transactions are taking place between digital systems — sometimes without a person involved at all.

This processing creates enormous economic value and opportunities. It also creates new opportunities for fraud, waste and abuse by bad actors and by companies. We cannot assume that every person, piece of information, system or action is trustworthy.

Trust needs to become something we can establish, verify and confirm and even audit.

Gwirio is building the trust infrastructure for our new digital world. We help people, companies and digital systems build stronger trust processes — so they can establish who or what can be trusted, what can be relied upon, what is permitted, and who is accountable. We are building trust to drive economic activity.

We may not think about trust when we buy something, share information, approve a transaction or rely on a digital service. We simply assume it. But as more of the digital world becomes automated, that assumption is no longer enough.

Gwirio helps make trust visible, verifiable and actionable — helping protect people, companies and our digital economy from fraud, waste and abuse.

And that is why we say “Trust precedes transactions.”

What we do

A platform for trust and data lifecycle management

Gwirio has developed and tested a platform for trust assertions, consent management, privacy management and data lifecycle management, including a Data Owner Portal and a Data Subject Portal for issuing and reviewing privacy certificates, revoking consent, and viewing issued certificates, with compliance reporting across multiple segments for a company. The full trust assertion process can be demonstrated on request.
