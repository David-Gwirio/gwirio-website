Insurance

An insurer often needs confirmation of a specific fact, not the full underlying record behind it.

The trust problem

Insurers perform their own risk assessment, KYC and trust scoring for each customer, often re-verifying facts that other authoritative organisations already hold.

What trusted outcome is required

An insurer may need confirmation that a driver's licence is valid, without a complete government record. It may need to know a device is the one normally associated with a customer, without access to the underlying network information a mobile operator holds.

This is a brief early treatment, based on the limited existing material on insurance. A fuller application page will be developed as more source material is available.
